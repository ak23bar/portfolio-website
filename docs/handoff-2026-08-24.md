## Decisions made this session (reverse them freely, but know they were deliberate)

1. **Five systems, not six.** Contrast among strong systems beat padding to a
   round number. Pocket Brain and the hls4ml fellowship stay in Matrix at full
   detail.
2. **Two cards carry no outbound link.** Measured, not assumed:
   `akbar.califorsale.org` (the MLS "live demo" still linked from Matrix) fails
   DNS entirely, and both `github.com/teklystudio/...` quant repos 404 and are
   described as private by the project's own record. A dead link is worse than
   none.
3. **About and capabilities were cut** on the owner's call late in the session.
   The degree fact from About survived as the closing line of experience. Their
   palette entries, stylesheet blocks, and mobile rules went with them.
4. **`#about` and `#capabilities` fragments no longer exist.** Old links to them
   land at the top of a page short enough that everything is one or two scrolls
   away. Three of the five original fragment ids survive.
5. **The resume is the factual authority.** AHEAD reads "Enterprise AI Intern"
   here; Matrix still says "Enterprise AI Engineering Intern". That drift is
   knowing, not accidental.
6. **Diagram pace lives in one token,** `--topo-cycle`. Every keyframe stop is a
   percentage, so changing that one value rescales travel, dwell, and both end
   pauses together and cannot break the one-active-layer invariant. Tune there,
   never in the keyframes.
